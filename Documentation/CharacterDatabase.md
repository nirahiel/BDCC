## Alex Rynard

### Background
- **Role**: Station Engineer
- **Species**: Fennec Fox
- **Personality**: 
  - Initially distant and work-focused
  - Values consent and trust highly
  - Struggles with depression and trust issues
  - Skilled engineer, particularly with electronics and prosthetics
- **Key Traits**:
  - Has a prosthetic spine (result of a past accident)
  - Created the prison's collar system
  - Works on various engineering projects, including experimental devices

### First Meeting
- **Location**: Mineshaft entrance
- **Context**: 
  - Player is assigned to mine work
  - Alex is supervising the mining operations
  - Initial interaction is professional and brief
  - First impression is of a no-nonsense engineer

### Relationships
- **The Director (Wright)**:
  - Complex relationship; the Director saved Alex's life after his accident
  - Feels indebted but also controlled by the Director
  - The Director keeps Alex busy with menial tasks to prevent him from working on personal projects

- **Eliza Quinn**:
  - Professional relationship
  - Eliza occasionally consults Alex on medical equipment
  - Some tension due to their different approaches to ethics

- **Player Character**:
  - Starts as a professional relationship (engineer-inmate)
  - Can develop into a friendship or romantic relationship based on player choices
  - Trust must be earned through actions, not just words

### Questline Summary
1. **Chapter 1: Introduction**
   - **Initial Meeting**:
     - First encounter occurs in the mineshaft where Alex is supervising
     - Conversation is brief and professional
   - **Engineering Workshop**:
     - Player must ask about lewd topics to progress (no actual lewd content occurs)
     - This conversation is required to unlock further interactions
     - Alex declines any romantic/sexual advances but becomes more open to conversation
   - **Watching Alex Work**:
     - After the initial conversation, player can watch Alex repair datapads
     - This leads to more interactions and character development
   - **Hypnovisor Project**:
     - Introduction to Alex's experimental device
     - Player can choose to participate in non-sexual testing
     - Focus on trust and consent throughout the process

2. **Chapter 2: Deeper Connections**
   - Continues the engineering-focused storyline
   - Optional trust exercises (can be skipped)
   - More personal interactions and character development

### Key Details
- **Content Warnings**:
  - Some dialogue references sexual topics but doesn't depict explicit content
  - All intimate interactions are optional and clearly telegraphed
  - Player can complete the main storyline without engaging in sexual content
- **Progression Requirements**:
  - Must ask about lewd topics in the first conversation to unlock further content
  - No actual sexual content is required to progress
  - Trust exercises are completely optional

### Quest Flow
1. First meeting in mineshaft (brief introduction)
2. Engineering workshop conversation (must mention lewd topics)
3. Watching Alex work (datapad repairs)
4. Introduction to hypnovisor project
5. Optional trust exercises (can be skipped)
6. Continued story progression in Chapter 2

## Socket

### Background
- **Full Name**: Socket Hex
- **Role**: Station Engineer
- **Species**: Fennec Fox
- **Personality**:
  - Friendly and approachable
  - Enthusiastic about engineering and technology
  - Somewhat naive and trusting
  - Casual about nudity and sexuality
  - Values consent and communication
- **Key Traits**:
  - Wears a revealing outfit by choice (armored boots, sleeves, and visor)
  - Has a bioengineered body (including her reproductive system)
  - Skilled in netrunning and cybersecurity
  - Enjoys upgrading and improving the station

### First Meeting
- **Location**: Engineering Workshop
- **Context**:
  - Recently hired as a new engineer
  - Doesn't seem to understand social norms about clothing
  - Eager to help and make friends
  - Offers to help upgrade the player's cell

### Relationships
- **Alex Rynard**:
  - Professional colleagues in the engineering department
  - Respects Alex's technical skills
  - More approachable and friendly than Alex

- **Player Character**:
  - Friendly and helpful towards the player
  - Can develop a closer relationship based on player choices
  - Open to casual intimacy if the player shows interest

- **Other Staff**:
  - Generally well-liked for her friendly demeanor
  - Sometimes causes minor workplace disruptions due to her casual attitude towards nudity

### Role on the Station
- Handles various engineering tasks and station maintenance
- Assists with technical issues and upgrades
- Can help the player with certain quests and tasks
- Provides a more lighthearted perspective compared to other staff members
